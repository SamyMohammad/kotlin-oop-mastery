
============================================================
  1. Overriding shop payments
============================================================
  Payment of 10000 | fee=0 total=10000
  Card *4242 - Payment of 10000 | fee=200 total=10200
  Cash on delivery - Payment of 10000 | fee=1500 total=11500
   >> total() is not open - every payment computes it the same way, using its own fee().

============================================================
  2. super builds on the parent
============================================================
  Discount:            0% off
  SeasonalDiscount:    10% off
  BlackFridayDiscount: 50% off
   >> BlackFriday = super.percent() * 5 = 10 * 5.

============================================================
  3. final override
============================================================
  A class extending BlackFridayDiscount could not override percent() again.
  BlackFridayDiscount itself is final (no `open`), so nobody can extend it anyway.
