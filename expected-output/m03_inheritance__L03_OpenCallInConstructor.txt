
============================================================
  1. The trap
============================================================
  Screen init calls render()
  ProductScreen.render(): title=null stock=0
  --- object finished, render() again ---
  ProductScreen.render(): title=Blue Pen stock=12
   >> First call saw title=null, stock=0 even though both are non-null vals.

============================================================
  2. The fix
============================================================
  SafeProductScreen.render(): title=Blue Pen
   >> Construct completely, then call open members. IntelliJ warns: 'Calling non-final function in constructor'.
